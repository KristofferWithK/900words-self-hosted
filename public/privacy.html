What a self-built 900words sends, and where

This copy of 900words was built from its source code by whoever runs it, not by 900words. It has no account and no cloud save. This page describes what the software does; it is not a promise about any particular copy.

On this device

Your progress and settings are stored on this device (in the browser's storage, or in the iPhone app's). A backup from Settings is a file you keep; the app never sends it anywhere. Your own AI key is stored in its own slot on this device and is never part of a backup.

When Casey plays

Casey needs an AI model to give and guess clues. Where her requests go depends on the choice under Settings → Casey's AI:

Gemma on this iPhone or computer: nothing leaves the device. The model itself is downloaded once from Hugging Face when you ask for it, and kept in the app (iPhone) or in this browser's storage (computer).

Your own AI key: the board's words, the round so far, your clue or guess, and any word you look up go to the AI service you set, with your key. That service's own terms and logging apply. In a browser on your own computer, requests to Ollama Cloud pass through the local server that serves the app, and go on to ollama.com.

Your own Casey server: the same information goes to the Casey server you set, which asks its AI service. A random installation ID goes with it so the server can limit how much one device asks. Anonymous usage counters (no identifier of any kind) go to that server too, unless you turn them off in Settings, and the app may ask whether you want to share more.

Nothing else

With no Casey server set, the app sends no usage counters and no shared data. It does not contact 900words' own servers.
