## 1. What the curriculum is

The curriculum is the teaching content of 900words, in every language and every
city, wherever it appears in this repository. It includes:

- the word lists and their translations, genders, forms and notes;
- the example sentences, stories, travel-guide chapters, lessons and grammar course;
- Casey's clue books, association matrices and indexes, authored clues and
  boards, board keys and deal indexes;
- the audio recordings of words, sentences, phrases and chapters;
- translations and glosses of any of the above.

It is found mainly in `src/data/`, `src/lang/*/`, `src/i18n/glosses/`,
`proxy/data/`, `public/audio/` and `public/chapter-timings.*.json`. Where a
file holds both program code and curriculum, the code is under the Mozilla

generated sound effects in `public/audio/ui/` are not curriculum; they are
code output under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
