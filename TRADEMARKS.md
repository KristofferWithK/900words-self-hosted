# The 900words name and brand

The code of 900words is open source. Its name and brand are not.

These belong to Kristoffer With K and are not licensed by the
[Mozilla Public License 2.0](LICENSE) (which says so in its section 2.3) or by
the [curriculum licence](CURRICULUM-LICENSE.md):

- the name **900words**;
- its logo and app icon (`public/icons/`, `ios/App/App/Assets.xcassets/`,
  `android/app/src/main/res/`);
- **Casey**, the suitcase with eyes: her name as 900words' companion, her look
  and her character;
- the look of 900words as a whole: its visual identity and store listings.

## What you may do

- Build and play this repository for yourself exactly as it is, name, icon
  and Casey included.
- Say truthfully that your project is based on 900words, or works with it.

## What needs permission

- Publishing an app, a build or a website under the 900words name, with its
  icon, or with Casey as its companion.
- Anything that suggests your project is 900words or is endorsed by it.

If you publish a build of your own changes, give it a new name and a new icon,
and do not use Casey as its companion. A published build also needs a
curriculum you are allowed to publish: see [CURRICULUM-LICENSE.md](CURRICULUM-LICENSE.md).

Ask through [900words.app](https://900words.app).
